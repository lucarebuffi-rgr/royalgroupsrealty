About Luca

A Trusted Name in
Cash Home Buying

My name is Luca Rebuffi and I founded Royal Groups Realty in 2020 with one goal: to give homeowners a faster, simpler, and more transparent way to sell — without the repairs, commissions, or months of waiting that come with a traditional sale.

Over the past 6 years I've closed 250+ transactions across Texas and Georgia, helping families navigate some of life's most challenging moments — divorce, foreclosure, probate, inherited properties, financial hardship, and more. Every situation is different, and I treat every seller with the respect and honesty they deserve.

Unlike large corporate buyers, when you call Royal Groups Realty, you get me directly. I personally evaluate your property, make the offer, and guide you through closing. There's no middleman, no bait-and-switch, and no last-minute price drops.

I openly share market data, comparable sales, and rehab numbers so you can make the best decision for your family — even if that means not selling to me. My business runs on referrals and reputation, and I take that seriously every single day.
