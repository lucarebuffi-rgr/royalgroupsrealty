Tired Landlord? Sell With Problem Tenants In Place

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  May 2025  ·  6 min read
The Reality

Being a Landlord Was Supposed to Be Passive Income

Being a landlord can be deeply rewarding — until it isn’t. Non-paying tenants, property damage, eviction court, and constant maintenance headaches wear down even the most patient investors. If you’ve hit your limit, you have options. You can sell your rental property with tenants still in place — without evicting anyone first. That’s exactly what we do.

Who We Help

Common Landlord Situations We Handle

  • Non-paying tenants you can’t evict fast enough
  • Tenants who have damaged the property
  • Properties tied up in eviction proceedings
  • Section 8 or subsidized housing landlords exiting
  • Multi-unit properties with mixed occupancy
  • Inherited rental properties you don’t want to manage
  • Out-of-state landlords tired of remote management
  • Portfolio landlords consolidating or exiting the market
Yes, You Can

Can You Sell a Rental With Tenants In Place?

Yes — and in many cases it’s easier than evicting first. Traditional retail buyers rarely want tenant-occupied properties, but cash investors buy them regularly. We purchase rental properties with tenants in place throughout Texas and Georgia, handling the tenant relationship ourselves after closing.

Texas

Texas Landlord-Tenant Laws

Texas has specific rules about tenant notification when a property is sold. As the seller, you’re responsible for notifying tenants of the sale. Security deposits must be transferred properly. We can guide you through this process and make it as smooth as possible for all parties.

Georgia

Georgia Landlord-Tenant Laws

Georgia similarly requires proper tenant notification. Security deposits must be transferred to the new owner at closing. We handle all of this as part of our purchase process in counties like Cobb, DeKalb, and Clayton.
