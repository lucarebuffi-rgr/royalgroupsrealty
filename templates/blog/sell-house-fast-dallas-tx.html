By Royal Groups Realty  ·  May 2025  ·  5 min read

If you need to sell your house fast in Dallas, TX, you're not alone. Thousands of Dallas homeowners every year find themselves in situations where a quick sale is the best — or only — option. Whether you're facing foreclosure, dealing with an inherited property, going through a divorce, or simply need to move fast, this guide covers everything you need to know.

Why Dallas Homeowners Need to Sell Fast

Dallas is one of the most dynamic real estate markets in the country, but speed isn't always guaranteed on the traditional market. Here are some of the most common reasons homeowners in Dallas reach out to cash buyers like Royal Groups Realty:

  • Facing foreclosure or behind on mortgage payments
  • Inherited a property they don't want to keep
  • Going through a divorce and need to liquidate assets
  • Relocating for work and need a quick close
  • Property needs major repairs they can't afford
  • Tired landlords looking to exit rental properties

Option 1: Sell to a Cash Buyer

Selling to a cash home buyer is the fastest way to sell a house in Dallas. Companies like Royal Groups Realty can make you an offer within 24 hours and close in as little as 7–28 days. Option 2: List with a Real Estate Agent

The traditional route — listing with a Realtor — can work well in a hot market, but it typically takes 30–90 days to close and comes with 5–6% in agent commissions, plus repair costs and closing fees. In a slower market or with a distressed property, this timeline can stretch even longer.

Option 3: For Sale By Owner (FSBO)

Selling on your own cuts out agent fees but requires significant time, marketing knowledge, and negotiation experience. Most FSBO sellers in Dallas end up taking longer to sell and accepting lower offers than agent-listed homes.
