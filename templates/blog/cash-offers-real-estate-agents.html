Cash Offers for Real Estate Agents — Fixer-Uppers, Off-Market Listings & Difficult Sellers

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  June 2025  ·  7 min read

If you're a real estate agent in Texas or Georgia, you already know the pattern. You sit down with a potential seller, you tour the property, and then you realize — this listing is going to be a headache. The kitchen's from 1985, the roof needs replacing, there's foundation movement in the back, or the seller just isn't willing to do any of the prep work it takes to compete on the open market. Or maybe the seller doesn't want it listed at all.

At Royal Groups Realty, we work with agents every week in exactly these situations. We buy fixer-uppers, off-market properties, and homes from sellers who need speed or privacy over top retail dollar. And the most important thing for you to know upfront: your commission is protected. Always.

Why Agents Bring Us In

You only have so much time. Listings that sit, fall through, or require months of repair coordination eat into your earnings and your reputation. Here are the most common situations where agents call us across Texas and Georgia:

  • Fixer-uppers that won't FHA or VA finance. The seller can't fund repairs and most retail buyers can't get financing on the property as-is.
  • Sellers who need to close fast. Job relocation, divorce, foreclosure timeline, estate settlement — the calendar matters more than the last $10K.
  • Off-market listings. Sellers who want privacy — high-net-worth clients, estates, sensitive family situations, or properties tied up in legal complications.
  • Inherited or probate properties. Out-of-state heirs, multiple beneficiaries, or estates that need a clean resolution without a 90-day listing.
  • Tenant-occupied rentals. Landlord clients who want out without going through evictions or lease handoffs.
  • Hoarder homes, fire damage, mold, foundation, or major deferred maintenance. Properties that simply aren't ready for the MLS and aren't worth your client's repair budget.
  • Failed listings. Homes that have been on the market 60+ days with no traction and a frustrated seller.

Your Commission Is Protected — Every Time

This is the question every agent asks first, so we'll answer it upfront. We pay agent commissions on every deal we close with you. If you have a signed listing agreement or a representation agreement, we honor it. We can structure the deal as either:

  • You stay listing agent of record — we close through your brokerage, your name on the deal, full commission paid at closing.
  • You represent the seller as a buyer-rep — common for off-market situations where there's no MLS listing.

Either way, your commission comes out of the closing — not the seller's pocket and not yours. We've worked with hundreds of agents across Texas and Georgia and we've never closed without paying the agent on the other side.

How the Process Works for Agents

  1. Send us the property — address, condition notes, any photos, and what the seller needs (timeline, price floor, special circumstances). Email or call.
  2. Cash offer in 24 hours — fair, based on real comps and condition. No algorithm, no lowball game.
  3. Present to your client — we can join the call or hand it off to you with a written offer. Whatever works best for your relationship.
  4. Sign contracts through your brokerage — your listing agreement, your commission terms, your closing process.
  5. Close in 7–21 days at a reputable title company. Commissions disbursed at closing.

Cash Sale vs. Repair-Then-List — When to Recommend Each

You know your market and your client better than anyone. Here's a quick framework we hear agents use when deciding whether to bring us in versus pushing a traditional listing:

Situation Cash Sale Traditional Listing
Repairs needed over $25K Strong fit Often net less after costs
Seller needs to close in 30 days 7–21 day close Too tight for most listings
Property in great condition Usually not the best fit MLS gets top dollar
Seller wants privacy / off-market Clean, discreet close MLS = public listing
Tenant-occupied rental We buy with tenants Eviction or lease issues
Foreclosure / probate / divorce Speed + certainty Timeline risk
Failed listing (60+ days) Reset the deal Already exhausted

Frequently Asked Questions — From Agents

How is the commission paid?

Through closing, just like any other transaction. Whatever's in your listing agreement or buyer-rep agreement is what you get paid. We've never closed without honoring the agent's commission.

Do you assign contracts or actually close?

We close. With our own funds. We do not wholesale or assign contracts to other buyers. The name on the contract is the name on the closing documents.

How quickly can you make an offer?

Within 24 hours of receiving the property details. Sometimes faster if it's a property type or county we work in frequently.

What if my client wants to counter the offer?

We're open to negotiation. Our first offer is fair, but if your client has specific concerns or wants to talk through the numbers, we'll work with you to find something everyone's comfortable with.

Do you cover closing costs?

Yes, we cover standard closing costs on our side. Your client walks away with the agreed amount minus any payoffs (mortgage, liens, taxes) that come out at closing.

Can we close at my preferred title company?

Yes. We work with title companies across Texas and Georgia. As long as they're licensed and reputable, we'll close wherever your client (or you) is most comfortable.

Do you require an inspection?

No inspection contingency. We typically do one walkthrough — sometimes virtually if the property is out of the way — and that's it. No re-trades after the offer is signed.

What if the property has tenants?

We buy as-is with tenants in place. Your seller doesn't need to evict or wait for a lease to end. We handle the tenant relationship from closing forward.
