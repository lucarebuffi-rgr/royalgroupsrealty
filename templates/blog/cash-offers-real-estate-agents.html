Cash Offers for Real Estate Agents

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  June 2025  ·  7 min read
Built for Agents

A Reliable Cash Buyer for the Listings Nobody Wants to Touch

If you’re a real estate agent in Texas or Georgia, you’ve probably had this experience: a seller calls with a property that’s just not MLS-ready. Fixer-upper, inherited, tenant-occupied, hoarder situation, foundation problems, code violations, tax delinquency. You know it’s a listing that’s going to sit for 90+ days and turn into a headache.

Or worse — it’s a client you’ve been building for months, but the property is going to cost them $30K to prep, and they don’t have it. So they don’t sell. And you get nothing.

Royal Groups Realty is the solution. We’re a direct cash buyer operating across all of Texas and Georgia, and we work with agents daily. Your commission is protected. Your seller gets a fast, clean exit. And you actually get paid on deals that would otherwise sit or die.

The Agent Value Prop

What Working With Us Actually Looks Like

Here’s how the typical agent partnership plays out:

  • You bring us the property. Email, text, or call. Any details you have on condition, seller motivation, and timeline.
  • We evaluate within 24 hours. Comps, condition, situation. We come back with a real cash offer — not a lowball opener with a plan to renegotiate later.
  • Your commission is in the contract. Written into the purchase agreement. Paid at closing directly from title. Non-negotiable on our end.
  • We handle the seller directly if you want us to. You stay in the loop but don’t have to babysit the deal.
  • We close in 7–21 days. No financing contingency, no appraisal risk, no last-minute buyer cold feet.
  • You get paid. Full commission at closing.

💡 Your commission is always protected. We’ve closed hundreds of deals with agents and we’ve never once asked one to reduce their commission to make a deal work. If the math doesn’t support the offer, we walk. We don’t squeeze.

Common Agent Scenarios

When Cash Buyers Save the Deal

The seller can’t afford to prep the home

You’ve run the numbers. The house needs $25K in work to list. Your seller doesn’t have $25K. Instead of losing the client, refer them to us. They get a fair cash offer, you get your commission, everyone walks away.

The listing has been on the MLS for 90+ days

Price drops aren’t working. Showings have stopped. Your seller is losing patience. A cash offer as a backup option resets the conversation — and often becomes the exit strategy.

Inherited property with multiple heirs

Six heirs, three states, one agent trying to coordinate a traditional sale. A cash close solves the coordination problem — digital signatures, mobile notaries, done in 14 days. Read: Probate Real Estate in Texas & Georgia.

Tenant-occupied rental with a hostile tenant

Traditional buyers won’t touch an occupied rental — especially with an eviction risk. We buy tenants and all, no eviction required before closing. Read: Tired Landlord? Sell With Problem Tenants Still In Place.

The inspection killed the retail deal

Buyer’s inspector flagged foundation, plumbing, or roof issues. Deal collapses. Now the seller’s stuck. We buy homes with disclosed defects — no inspection contingency, no re-negotiation. Read: Selling With Foundation Issues.

Out-of-state owner who inherited a property

They don’t want to fly in for showings. They don’t want to manage renovations remotely. They just want it gone. We do everything remotely — digital signatures, mobile notary in their state. You get your commission without traveling.
