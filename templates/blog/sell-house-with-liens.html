Selling a House With Liens — HOA, City, Contractor & More

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  May 2025  ·  6 min read

A lien on your property doesn't have to stop you from selling. In fact, in most cases, liens are simply paid off through the title company at closing using the proceeds from the sale. Here's everything you need to know about selling a house with liens in Texas and Georgia.

Common Types of Property Liens

  • Mortgage liens: Your primary and any secondary mortgages
  • Property tax liens: Unpaid property taxes (automatic lien in TX and GA)
  • HOA liens: Unpaid homeowner association dues
  • Mechanic's liens: Unpaid contractor or subcontractor work
  • Judgment liens: Court judgments from creditors
  • IRS tax liens: Federal tax debts
  • City/code violation liens: Unpaid fines from code enforcement

How Liens Are Handled at Closing

When you sell your home, the title company conducts a title search to find all liens. At closing, each lien is paid off in order of priority using the sale proceeds. Whatever remains after all liens are paid goes to you. This means you don't need to pay off liens out of pocket before selling — they're resolved through the sale itself.

What If Liens Exceed the Home's Value?

If the total of all liens plus your mortgage exceeds what the home is worth, you're in a negative equity ("underwater") situation. Options include a short sale (negotiating with lenders to accept less), filing for bankruptcy, or negotiating lien reductions directly with creditors. Read: Selling During Financial Hardship or Bankruptcy.

HOA Liens in Texas and Georgia

HOA liens can be surprisingly aggressive in both states. In Texas, HOAs have the right to foreclose on super-priority liens. In Georgia, HOA liens must typically be satisfied before a clear title can be issued. Either way, we handle these at closing.

Code Violation Liens

If your city has issued fines or placed liens for code violations, those are also resolved at closing. For properties with active code enforcement actions, read: Selling a House With Code Violations.
