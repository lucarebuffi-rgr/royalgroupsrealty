Selling Your House As-Is in DFW — What to Expect

By Royal Groups Realty  ·  May 2025  ·  5 min read

Selling a house as-is in the Dallas-Fort Worth area means selling without making any repairs, updates, or improvements before the sale. It's an increasingly popular option for DFW homeowners who want speed, simplicity, and certainty over top dollar. Here's everything you need to know.

What Does "As-Is" Actually Mean?

In real estate, selling as-is means the seller will not make repairs or issue credits for deficiencies found during inspection. The buyer accepts the property in its current condition. In Texas, sellers are still required to disclose known material defects — selling as-is doesn't mean hiding problems.

Why DFW Homeowners Sell As-Is

  • The property needs significant repairs they can't afford or don't want to manage
  • They inherited the property and don't know its full condition
  • They need to close quickly and don't have time for renovations
  • The property has been a rental and has tenant damage
  • They're going through foreclosure, divorce, or financial hardship

As-Is on the Open Market vs Selling to a Cash Buyer

You can list an as-is home on the MLS in DFW, but expect a smaller buyer pool, lower offers, and buyers who still request inspections and price reductions. Most traditional buyers need financing — and banks are reluctant to lend on distressed properties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I get less money selling as-is in DFW?

An as-is offer will typically be below full retail value — but when you factor in the cost of repairs, agent commissions, and carrying costs during a longer sale, the net difference is often much smaller than expected.

Do I need to disclose problems if I sell as-is in Texas?

Yes. Texas requires sellers to disclose known material defects regardless of whether you're selling as-is. Selling as-is means you won't fix the problems — not that you can hide them.
