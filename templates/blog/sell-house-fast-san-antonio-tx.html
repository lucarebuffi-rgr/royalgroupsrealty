How to Sell a House Fast in San Antonio, TX

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  September 2026  ·  7 min read
The Reality

A Big Market Doesn't Always Mean a Fast Sale

San Antonio's size works against you if you need to sell quickly. Historic homes near downtown and Alamo Heights come with age-related repairs, military relocations out of JBSA-Lackland, JBSA-Fort Sam Houston, and JBSA-Randolph happen on tight timelines, and a crowded resale market means your listing competes with hundreds of others at any given time.

We buy houses throughout San Antonio and Bexar County — as-is, for cash, with no repairs and no commissions.

How It Works

A Simple, Fast Process

  1. Tell us about the property. Address, condition, and why you're selling.
  2. Get a cash offer within 24 hours. Based on recent comparable sales in your specific San Antonio neighborhood.
  3. Pick your closing date. As fast as 7 days, or longer if you need time to coordinate a PCS move or estate matters.
  4. Close and get paid. No repairs, no commissions, no financing contingencies to worry about.

💡 We regularly work with military families relocating from JBSA installations and are familiar with the documentation and timeline a PCS move requires.
