How to Sell a House Fast in Austin, TX

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  September 2026  ·  7 min read
The Reality

Austin's Market Has Cooled — And That Changes the Math

Austin spent years as one of the hottest housing markets in the country, but the past couple of years have told a different story: inventory sitting longer, price reductions becoming common, and buyers who can afford to be picky about condition. If your home needs work, is tied up in an estate, or you simply need to sell on your own timeline rather than waiting out a slower market, the traditional listing route doesn't carry the guarantees it used to.

We buy houses throughout Austin and all of Travis and Williamson counties — as-is, for cash, with no repairs and no commissions.

How It Works

A Simple, Fast Process

  1. Tell us about the property. Address, condition, and why you're selling — takes about five minutes.
  2. Get a cash offer within 24 hours. Based on recent comparable sales in your specific Austin neighborhood, not a citywide average.
  3. Pick your closing date. As fast as 7 days, or longer if you need time to move out or coordinate an estate.
  4. Close and get paid. No repairs, no commissions, no waiting on a buyer's financing to clear.

💡 We handle title issues, back taxes, liens, and inherited property paperwork as part of the process — you don't have to sort any of it out before calling us.

Market Context

Why "Just List It" Doesn't Work Like It Used To

During Austin's peak years, almost any property sold quickly regardless of condition, because buyer demand outpaced supply by a wide margin. That's no longer the environment. With more inventory on the market and buyers able to be selective, homes needing real work — foundation issues, outdated systems, deferred maintenance — now sit far longer than move-in-ready properties, and often go through multiple price cuts before finding a buyer willing to take on the repairs.

For sellers who don't want to gamble on that timeline, or who don't have the cash to make a property market-ready first, a direct cash sale removes the uncertainty entirely.
