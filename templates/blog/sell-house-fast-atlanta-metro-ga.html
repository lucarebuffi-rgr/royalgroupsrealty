The Process — Simple From Start to Finish

Whether you’re in Lawrenceville, Newnan, Conyers, Canton, or anywhere else in the Atlanta metro, the process is the same and takes as little as 7 days from first contact to cash in hand:

  1. Contact us — fill out the form on our website or call 469-665-8481. We respond the same day.
  2. We evaluate your property — no in-person visit required in most cases. We base our offer on your property details and local market data.
  3. Cash offer within 24 hours — fair, no-obligation, based on real Atlanta metro comps. No pressure to accept.

Selling an Inherited Home

Inherited properties are one of the most common situations we handle across the Atlanta metro. Whether the home is in Gwinnett, Cobb, or a smaller suburban county, inherited properties often come with complications — outdated condition, out-of-state heirs, probate proceedings, or title issues. We work with executors, attorneys, and heirs to close quickly and cleanly. Learn more: Selling an Inherited Home in Georgia and Probate Real Estate in Georgia.

Facing Foreclosure in an Atlanta Suburb

If you’re behind on your mortgage in Gwinnett, Henry, Rockdale, or any other metro Atlanta county, a cash sale may be your best option to stop the foreclosure clock before an auction date damages your credit. Time matters — the sooner you act, the more options you have. Read more: How to Avoid Foreclosure.

Selling a Rental Property

Atlanta’s rental market has seen rising tenant disputes and eviction complications across suburban counties.
