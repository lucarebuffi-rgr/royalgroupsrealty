How to Sell a Hoarder House Fast — Discreetly

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  June 2025  ·  8 min read
Read This First

If You’re Reading This About Someone You Love, You’re Not Alone

Hoarding is one of the most misunderstood situations in real estate — and one of the most emotionally exhausting for the families involved. If you’re dealing with a parent’s home, a sibling’s home, or your own space, and you’re trying to figure out what to do with the property, you’re not alone. About 2–6% of the U.S. population struggles with some form of hoarding — and among older adults, it’s higher.

This isn’t about judgment. It’s about giving you a path forward. Royal Groups Realty buys hoarder homes as-is across Texas and Georgia. We don’t require cleanup. We don’t list photos publicly. We don’t treat sellers or their families like a problem. We just buy the house, handle the cleanout after closing, and get you a clean exit.

How We Handle It

What Makes Our Approach Different

  • No cleanup required. Not even a little. Take what’s meaningful — photos, jewelry, personal papers — and leave everything else.
  • No public listings, no marketing photos. We don’t post the property, don’t share pictures, don’t assign the deal to another buyer.
  • Full cleanout after closing at no cost to you. We handle everything — furniture, belongings, biohazard cleanup if needed.
  • Discreet neighbors-first process. No dumpsters in the driveway during the sale, no signs, no drive-bys from other buyers.
  • Respectful with the person involved. If a parent or family member is still living in the home, we treat them with dignity, not as an obstacle.
  • Fast closing. 7–21 days once we’re under contract. Often faster if there’s urgency.

💡 You will never receive a text from us asking if we can “send some pictures to our team.” We know how much shame surrounds this situation. Everything we do respects that.

The Reality

Why Selling a Hoarder House on the MLS Almost Never Works

1. Cleanout costs are extreme

Professional hoarder cleanout services in Texas and Georgia typically run $5,000–$25,000 depending on severity, biohazard level, and square footage. In extreme cases with animal waste, deceased occupants, or structural damage from years of accumulation, costs can exceed $50,000.

2. Interior damage is almost always present

Years of accumulation cause hidden damage — water damage from blocked appliances, pest infestations, rotted subflooring, damaged drywall, HVAC systems ruined by dust and dander. This damage isn’t always visible until cleanout begins, and it typically adds another $10,000–$30,000 of repair costs before an MLS listing is possible.

3. Retail buyers walk during inspection

Even after cleanout and cosmetic repair, hoarder homes often have persistent odor and residual damage that inspectors flag immediately. Deals fall through at high rates.

4. Insurance issues

Many carriers won’t write policies on homes with recent hoarding history. This eliminates most retail buyers who need financing.

5. The emotional cost is enormous

Even if the financial math worked out — which it usually doesn’t — the process of clearing out a parent’s or sibling’s home over weeks or months is devastating. Most families we work with have been dreading this for years. Handing it off in a 21-day close is often the best gift they can give themselves.
