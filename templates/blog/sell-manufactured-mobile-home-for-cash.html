How to Sell a Manufactured or Mobile Home for Cash

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  October 2026  ·  6 min read
The Reality

Manufactured Homes Are Harder to Sell Than Site-Built Houses

Most real estate agents don't list manufactured or mobile homes, and most buyers can't get a traditional mortgage for one — especially older units or homes on leased land. That combination leaves a lot of owners stuck with a property they can't easily sell through normal channels, even when the home itself is in decent shape.

What We Buy

Any Situation, Any Land Arrangement

We buy manufactured and mobile homes regardless of how the land underneath them is set up:

  • Homes on owned land (the home and the land sold together)
  • Homes on leased land (park or private lot lease)
  • Homes inside a manufactured home community or park
  • Older, pre-HUD-code manufactured homes (built before June 1976)
  • Homes with missing or unclear title paperwork

💡 Not sure if your home is titled as real property or personal property (chattel)? That distinction affects the sale process, and we help sort it out as part of making an offer — you don't need to figure it out yourself first.

Why It's Different

Real Property vs. Personal Property (Chattel)

A manufactured home can be titled one of two ways: as real property (converted and taxed like a site-built house, usually when it's permanently affixed to owned land) or as personal property, sometimes called chattel (titled more like a vehicle, common for homes in parks or on leased land). Chattel loans are harder for buyers to qualify for, have higher interest rates, and fewer lenders offer them at all — which is a major reason these homes sit unsold longer than site-built houses.

The Process

How We Buy Your Manufactured or Mobile Home

  1. Tell us about the home and land situation. Whether it's on owned land, leased land, or in a park, and its approximate age and condition.
  2. We verify the title status. Real property or personal property/chattel — this determines the paperwork needed to transfer ownership.
  3. We make a cash offer. Accounting for the home's age, condition, and land situation.
  4. We close with the correct title transfer process. Whether that's a standard deed or a manufactured home title transfer through your state's DMV or housing authority equivalent.
