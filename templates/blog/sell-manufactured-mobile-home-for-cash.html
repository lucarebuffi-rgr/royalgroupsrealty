How to Sell a Manufactured or Mobile Home on Land You Own

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  October 2026  ·  6 min read
The Reality

Manufactured Homes Are Harder to Sell Than Site-Built Houses

Most real estate agents don't list manufactured or mobile homes, and financing for them is harder for buyers to get than a standard mortgage. If you own a manufactured or mobile home on your own land — not a leased lot, not a rental space in a park — we can make selling it as simple as selling a site-built house.

What We Buy

The Home and Land, Sold Together

We purchase manufactured and mobile homes only when the seller owns the land underneath them. The home and the land are sold together as a single real estate transaction, the same way we'd buy any site-built house and lot. This includes:

  • Single-wide or double-wide homes on owned land, any acreage
  • Homes already converted and titled as real property
  • Homes that haven't been converted to real property yet — we help work through that as part of closing
  • Older, pre-HUD-code manufactured homes (built before June 1976), as long as the land is owned
  • Homes needing repairs, storm damage, or significant deferred maintenance

⚠️ Important: We do not purchase manufactured or mobile homes on leased land, rented lots, or land-lease spaces inside a manufactured home community or park. The land has to be owned along with the home for us to make an offer.

Why Owned Land Matters

Real Property vs. Personal Property (Chattel)

A manufactured home can be titled one of two ways: as real property (converted and taxed like a site-built house, which requires owning the land it sits on) or as personal property, sometimes called chattel (titled more like a vehicle, common for homes on leased lots or in parks). We buy in the first situation — home and land together as real estate — which is what lets us close with a standard deed transfer instead of a separate vehicle-style title process.

If your home hasn't been formally converted to real property yet but you do own the land, that's not a problem — we help walk through that conversion as part of the sale.

The Process

How We Buy Your Manufactured or Mobile Home

  1. Tell us about the home and confirm you own the land. We only make offers when the land is owned outright by the seller, not leased.
  2. We verify land ownership and title status. This confirms whether the home is already titled as real property or still needs conversion.
  3. We make a cash offer for the home and land together. Based on the home's age, condition, and the land itself.
  4. We close with a standard real estate deed. The same process as any other property sale, since the home and land transfer as one.
