How to Sell a House Fast in Marietta, GA

By Luca Rebuffi – Royal Groups Realty  ·  June 2025  ·  6 min read

Marietta is one of Metro Atlanta's most desirable cities — a charming historic square, top-rated schools, Truist Park right around the corner, and easy access to downtown Atlanta. But even in a strong market like Marietta, life doesn't always give you time to wait 60–90 days for the right retail buyer. If you need to sell your Marietta home fast, this guide covers exactly what your options are and how to get the best outcome.

Why Marietta Homeowners Sell Fast

Across Cobb County, we work with homeowners in all kinds of situations where speed matters more than squeezing every dollar from a traditional listing:

  • Inherited a home and want a quick, clean resolution
  • Going through a divorce and need to split assets fast
  • Relocating for work and can't manage a property from out of state
  • Behind on mortgage payments or property taxes
  • Home needs significant repairs that aren't worth investing in
  • Tired landlord dealing with problem tenants
  • Estate sale with multiple heirs who need to move on

The Marietta Real Estate Market in 2025

Marietta and the broader Cobb County market remain competitive, but conditions have shifted. Interest rate increases have slowed buyer demand, meaning homes that need work or are priced aggressively are sitting longer than they were in 2021–2022. For sellers who need certainty — not hope — a cash offer is often the smartest move in today's market.

The Marietta market spans a wide range — from older ranch-style homes in East Cobb to newer construction near Smyrna and Vinings. Whatever your property type, Royal Groups Realty evaluates every home based on its specific condition and neighborhood, not a generic algorithm.

Your Options for Selling Fast in Marietta

Option 1: Sell to a Cash Buyer (Fastest)

A direct cash sale to Royal Groups Realty eliminates every friction point of a traditional sale. No showings, no repairs, no open houses, no financing contingencies. We can close in as little as 7 days — or on whatever timeline works for you. There are zero agent commissions and zero closing costs. What we offer is what you walk away with.

Wondering how this compares financially to a traditional sale? Read our breakdown: Cash Offer vs. Traditional Home Sale.

Option 2: List With a Realtor

If your home is in excellent condition and you have 2–4 months, listing with an agent in Marietta can yield strong results. But factor in the 5–6% commission, potential repair requests from buyers, appraisal gaps, and the possibility of deals falling through — it's a longer, less certain process.

Option 3: iBuyer Platforms

Companies like Opendoor operate in the Atlanta market but typically charge service fees of 5–8%, require the home to meet certain condition standards, and make algorithmic offers that don't account for your property's unique strengths. Many Marietta sellers find our offers more competitive once all fees are factored in.

Frequently Asked Questions — Selling in Marietta, GA

How fast can you close on my Marietta home?

We can close in as little as 7 days in Marietta. Most closings happen within 14–28 days depending on title work and your preferred timeline. We work with a trusted Georgia title company to make the process smooth.

Do I need to make repairs before selling my Marietta home?

Absolutely not. We buy homes in any condition throughout Cobb County — outdated kitchens, aging HVAC, foundation concerns, cosmetic issues, fire or water damage. Leave it exactly as it is.

How do you calculate your cash offer for Marietta homes?

We base our offer on your home's condition, location within Marietta, recent comparable sales in your specific neighborhood, and current Cobb County market trends. We share all of this data openly — no black-box algorithms.

Are there any fees or commissions?

None. We cover all closing costs. The cash offer we make is exactly what you receive at the closing table.

Can you buy my Marietta home if it has liens or back taxes?

Yes. Liens and delinquent taxes are paid off at closing through the title company. You don't need to resolve them out of pocket before we can close. Learn more: Selling a House With Liens.
